# Step 9 consequential — model notes

**Do not open this until judgments are recorded on
`docs/step9_consequential_check.md`.** These notes explain why the
model chose the label. Reading them first anchors agreement.

## 1. NCT05902988 — tumour genetic marker `barred`

The trial excludes these listed tumor molecular features and does not state any required tumor genetic marker.

## 2. NCT06561386 — tumour genetic marker `barred`

PD-L1 expression is not recorded as a genetic marker here. The trial excludes specified actionable tumor alterations and also excludes unknown EGFR/ALK/ROS-1 status, but unknown status is not itself a genetic marker.

## 3. NCT07696832 — tumour genetic marker `barred`

The trial excludes patients with the listed actionable driver alterations; although inclusion mentions examples like EGFR exon 21 insertions, c-MET aberrations, BRAF V600E, HER2, and KRAS G12C as permitted in some circumstances, it does not require those markers for enrollment.

## 4. NCT06947694 — tumour genetic marker `barred`

The trial excludes tumors positive for the listed driver genes, but does not specify particular mutation/fusion/rearrangement/amplification variants within those genes.

## 5. NCT05800223 — tumour genetic marker `required`

The trial requires EGFR exon 19 deletion or EGFR exon 21 L858R substitution mutation. The exclusion text also refuses patients with “NSCLC EGFR driver gene negative.”

## 6. NCT05037331 — tumour genetic marker `required`

The trial requires activating EGFR sensitising mutations; the quote also explicitly allows patients with or without T790M resistance mutation, so T790M is not strictly required on its own but is named within the required mutation clause.

## 7. NCT07064018 — tumour genetic marker `required`

The only explicitly required tumour genetic marker is KRAS mutated, and it is required only for the pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma subgroup. The exclusion about 'known driver mutations' is too general to name any specific refused marker.

## 8. NCT06764771 — tumour genetic marker `required`

KRAS G12C mutation is required only for the specified study parts; no genetic marker exclusions are stated in the provided text.

## 9. NCT07626736 — disease stage `barred`

The trial explicitly excludes Stage I patients but does not explicitly state which other stages are accepted.

## 10. NCT05468242 — disease stage `required`

Prior platinum-based chemotherapy is implicitly permitted for the Tislelizumab consolidation phase because only patients who progressed during definitive platinum based concurrent chemoradiation are excluded.

## 11. NCT06532149 — disease stage `required`

The trial includes advanced/metastatic NSCLC and mentions prior cisplatin only in the context of excluding participants not adequately recovered from chemotherapy-induced gonadotoxicity.

## 12. NCT06890598 — disease stage `required`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is only explicitly mentioned for Part B as concurrent platinum-based chemoradiotherapy without progression.

## 13. NCT05344209 — disease stage `required`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is not named specifically, but prior systemic neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy is allowed under a time limit, which would include platinum-containing chemotherapy if given in that setting.

## 14. NCT06514027 — disease stage `required`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is not named specifically, but prior systemic treatment for the eligible advanced/recurrent/metastatic NSCLC is excluded, with an exception for earlier neoadjuvant/adjuvant therapy completed at least 6 months earlier.

## 15. NCT06931717 — disease stage `required`

The trial excludes any prior neoadjuvant or adjuvant systemic treatment for NSCLC, which includes prior platinum chemotherapy for NSCLC; it also explicitly discusses adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy only as treatment patients are not candidates for, not as prior treatment.

## 16. NCT07444814 — disease stage `required`

The text names a tumor type as 'Platinum Resistant Ovarian Cancer,' which implies prior platinum exposure but does not explicitly state prior platinum chemotherapy as an eligibility requirement for an individual participant.

## 17. NCT05766800 — previous platinum chemotherapy `barred`

The prior-treatment exclusion is broad and not specific to platinum drugs, so platinum chemotherapy is inferred only as part of the barred systemic anti-cancer treatment history.

## 18. NCT06692738 — previous platinum chemotherapy `barred`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is not named specifically, but because any prior systemic non-curative therapy for NSCLC is excluded, prior platinum chemotherapy for NSCLC would be barred.

## 19. NCT06838273 — previous platinum chemotherapy `barred`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is not named specifically, but prior systemic therapy is excluded, which would include prior platinum chemotherapy.

## 20. NCT04282044 — previous platinum chemotherapy `required`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is specifically required only for subjects with ovarian cancer.

## 21. NCT05696782 — previous platinum chemotherapy `required`

Prior platinum chemotherapy is required, including if currently being administered or completed within the last 14 days; autoimmune disease is excluded only when active and requiring systemic immunosuppression, with a separate exclusion for prior autoimmune pneumonitis requiring high-dose steroids.

## 22. NCT07050043 — previous immunotherapy `barred`

Prior immunotherapy is explicitly excluded. Brain/meningeal metastases are not universally excluded because some participants with such metastases may enroll if specific stability criteria are met, while carcinomatous meningitis is excluded.

## 23. NCT07410975 — previous immunotherapy `barred`

The trial explicitly excludes any prior immunotherapy as part of banning prior anticancer therapy, and it does not mention brain metastases or CNS/leptomeningeal disease.

## 24. NCT06598527 — previous immunotherapy `barred`

The trial explicitly excludes prior checkpoint/immunoregulatory therapy, but the mention of brain MRI is only for baseline staging and does not state whether patients with brain metastases are eligible or ineligible.

## 25. NCT07158489 — previous immunotherapy `required`

Prior immunotherapy is explicitly required as one of the prior regimens; brain/CNS metastases are generally excluded when symptomatic, but treated and stable CNS metastases are allowed under stated conditions.

## 26. NCT06626243 — previous immunotherapy `required`

Prior immunotherapy is explicitly required by the inclusion criteria, while brain metastases or other CNS involvement are not mentioned in the provided text.

## 27. NCT05098132 — cancer spread to the brain `barred`

Prior immunotherapy is explicitly excluded in Phase 2. Brain/CNS involvement is excluded via active central nervous system metastases and any history of carcinomatous meningitis.

## 28. NCT07774520 — cancer spread to the brain `barred`

The trial excludes participants with CNS/leptomeningeal metastasis, and it does not specifically mention prior immunotherapy or other prior PD-1/PD-L1/CTLA-4 treatment.

## 29. NCT05033691 — cancer spread to the brain `required`

Brain metastases are required for enrollment, but the trial also limits them to asymptomatic or minor symptoms and under 20 lesions; leptomeningeal disease is separately excluded.

## 30. NCT07056556 — autoimmune disease `barred`

The trial allows locally advanced or metastatic disease in the listed tumor types, conditioned on having failed standard treatment; prior platinum chemotherapy is not specifically mentioned.
