# Five extra invented patients — vague treatment history

**These are invented. No real patient data was used.** They exist only to test
the safety valve: a patient whose note says "chemotherapy" or "systemic
therapy" without naming a drug must not be narrowed by a trial whose rule
names platinum specifically.

The original 20 in `data/fake_patients.md` all name a specific drug or a
specific class (carboplatin, pembrolizumab, platinum-based, checkpoint
inhibitor, and so on). None of them is this case.

> chemotherapy. No CNS disease. ECOG 1, CrCl 80.
